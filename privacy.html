Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Start/Line is a training and nutrition planner for runners and triathletes, on the web and on iPhone. This page explains what it stores and how to remove it.

What Start/Line stores

  • Your account: your name, email address and password. The password is stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider. We never see it.
  • What you enter: your races, training days, and the health details you give for your plan, such as age, sex, height, weight, fitness level, diet, allergies, and any injuries or conditions you choose to note. Also your food list and grocery settings.

If you use Start/Line without an account, all of this stays on your device and is never sent to us.

How it's used

Your details are used only to build your training plan, nutrition plan and grocery list, and to keep them in sync between your devices. We don't sell your data, show ads, use analytics or tracking, or share your data with anyone else.

Where it's kept

Accounts and saved plans are stored with Supabase, which hosts our database and sign-in. Each person's data is protected so that only they can read or change it. Supabase sends our account emails, such as sign-up confirmation and password reset.

Deleting your data

Sign in, then tap Delete account at the top of the app and tap again to confirm. This permanently deletes your account and everything saved with it. To remove data saved without an account, clear the app's data or delete the app.

Children

Start/Line is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Health information

Start/Line gives general guidance, not medical advice. It doesn't connect to Apple Health or any other health service.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. For questions or support, contact us here.
